Kikis Auto Captions

1. Choose your clip

Browse to your exported clip.

2. Generate transcript

This listens to the clip and writes down every word with its timing. You'll be able to review and fix it before anything gets burned in.







  
3. Review the transcript

  
Click any word to fix it (e.g. if it misheard something). Changes save automatically and are used for both the preview and the final burn.

  






  
4. Style your captions

  
This preview is rendered the same way the final video is, so what you see here is what you'll get. Drag the caption to reposition it.

  

    
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  

  

    
    
    
    
  

  

    
    
  

  
Canvas size is only used for the transparent overlay export below — the burned-in video always renders at your source clip's real resolution.


  

    
Per-segment style overrides (optional)

    
E.g. a different highlight color for each speaker. Enter start/end in seconds — hover a word in the transcript above to see its timestamp. If ranges overlap, the earlier one wins. Segments are per-clip and reset when you start a new transcription.

    

    
  


  

    
Scrub the actual clip

    
Play or drag through the real clip below. The caption shown is an approximate live overlay (not pixel-exact — trust the still preview and the real burn for exact look) that updates shortly after you pause or stop scrubbing, tracking whatever style/segment/chunk settings are currently set.

5. Burn captions in

Kikis Auto Captions

1. Choose your clip

Browse to your exported clip.

2. Generate transcript

This listens to the clip and writes down every word with its timing. You'll be able to review and fix it before anything gets burned in.







  
3. Review the transcript

  
Click any word to fix it (e.g. if it misheard something). Changes save automatically and are used for both the preview and the final burn.

  






  
4. Style your captions

  
This preview is rendered the same way the final video is, so what you see here is what you'll get. Drag the caption to reposition it.
